Loaded Chicken Sausage Breakfast Potato Bowl

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Cook Time: 15 minutes

Servings: 1

Serving Size: 1 breakfast bowl

Macros Per Serving: 395 calories | 41g protein | 30g carbohydrates | g sugar | g fiber | 16g fat

Behind the Recipe

A hearty tortilla-free breakfast bowl with crispy potatoes and enough protein to carry a busy morning.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Simply Potatoes Diced Potatoes
  • 2 Amylu Breakfast Time Chicken Sausage Links, sliced
  • 1 large egg
  • 2/3 cup liquid egg whites
  • 1/4 cup Daisy 4% Cottage Cheese
  • 1 tablespoon reduced-fat shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tablespoons salsa
  • 1 tablespoon sliced green onion
  • Nonstick cooking spray
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Lightly coat a large nonstick skillet with cooking spray and heat over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the diced potatoes. Season with salt and pepper, then cook for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned and crispy.
  3. Add the sliced chicken sausage and cook for 2 to 3 minutes, until heated through and lightly browned. Transfer the potatoes and sausage to a bowl.
  4. Blend the egg, egg whites, and cottage cheese until completely smooth.
  5. Reduce the skillet to medium-low heat and lightly coat it with cooking spray. Pour in the egg mixture and cook slowly, stirring gently, until the eggs are soft and fully set.
  6. Spoon the eggs over the potatoes and sausage. Sprinkle the cheddar over the hot eggs.
  7. Top with the salsa and sliced green onion. Add additional salt and pepper to taste.

40/400 Notes

  • Cook the eggs over medium-low heat for a soft texture.
  • Nutrition varies by sausage, cottage cheese, cheddar, and salsa brand.
