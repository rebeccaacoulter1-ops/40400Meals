Caramel Pecan Cottage Cheese Bowl

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Cook Time: 1 minutes

Servings: 1

Serving Size: 1 bowl, about 1 1/2 cups

Macros Per Serving: 400 calories | 42g protein | 29g carbohydrates | 9g sugar | 4g fiber | 14g fat

Behind the Recipe

Savory cottage cheese and sweet caramel play off each other here, with a crumbled Built Puff bar adding the crunch and pumpkin-cookie flavor of the season.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • 2 tablespoons chopped pecans
  • 1 tablespoon Splenda brown sugar blend (optional, for candying the pecans)
  • 2 tablespoons sugar-free caramel sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1 Built Puff Pumpkin Cookie Chunk bar, crumbled

Instructions

  1. Optional upgrade: toss the chopped pecans with the Splenda brown sugar blend in a small microwave-safe bowl and microwave for 30 to 45 seconds, stirring halfway, until fragrant. Let cool for 1 minute so they crisp up.
  2. Scoop the cottage cheese into a bowl and sprinkle with the cinnamon.
  3. Top with the pecans (candied or plain), drizzle with the sugar-free caramel sauce, and finish with the crumbled Built Puff bar.
  4. Serve right away for the best crunch.

40/400 Notes

  • Short on time? Skip the candying step — plain chopped pecans still taste great here.
  • Crumble the Built Puff bar just before serving so it stays crunchy instead of softening in the fridge.
  • Warm the caramel sauce for about 10 seconds if you want it extra drizzly.
