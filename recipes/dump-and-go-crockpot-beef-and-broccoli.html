Dump-and-Go Crockpot Beef and Broccoli

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Cook Time: 480 minutes

Servings: 6

Serving Size: Approximately 12 ounces, including 3/4 cup rice medley

Macros Per Serving: 380 calories | 40g protein | 31g carbohydrates | g sugar | g fiber | 10g fat

Behind the Recipe

Pre-sliced beef makes the morning genuinely dump-and-go. At night, frozen broccoli cooks in the thickened sauce while a frozen jasmine-rice and cauliflower-rice medley heats in the microwave.

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds pre-sliced beef stir-fry strips
  • 1 cup Simple Truth Organic Beef Bone Broth
  • 1/2 cup Kikkoman Less Sodium Soy Sauce
  • 2 tablespoons stevia brown-sugar substitute
  • 1 tablespoon Marukan Rice Vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon Shirakiku 100% Pure Sesame Oil
  • 2 teaspoons minced garlic
  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 2 tablespoons cold water
  • 1 (12.6-ounce) bag Birds Eye Baby Broccoli Florets
  • 1 (10-ounce) bag Birds Eye Steamfresh Original Riced Cauliflower
  • 1 (10-ounce) pouch Kroger Classic Grains Jasmine Rice from the freezer section

Instructions

  1. In the morning, place the pre-sliced beef stir-fry strips in a 6-quart crockpot.
  2. Add the beef bone broth, Kikkoman soy sauce, stevia brown-sugar substitute, Marukan rice vinegar, Shirakiku sesame oil, minced garlic, ground ginger, and black pepper. Stir to coat the beef.
  3. Cover and cook on LOW for 7 to 8 hours, until the beef is tender.
  4. At night, whisk the cornstarch and cold water together until smooth, then stir the slurry into the crockpot.
  5. Add the frozen Birds Eye broccoli, cover, and cook on HIGH for 20 to 30 minutes, until the broccoli is tender and the sauce thickens.
  6. Cook the Birds Eye riced cauliflower and one Kroger frozen jasmine-rice pouch according to their package directions.
  7. Combine the cooked jasmine rice and cauliflower rice to make the rice medley.
  8. Divide the beef-and-broccoli mixture among six bowls and serve each with approximately 3/4 cup rice medley.

40/400 Notes

  • Pre-sliced beef keeps morning preparation under five minutes.
  • Do not add the broccoli in the morning because it will become too soft during an all-day cook.
  • The Kroger rice package contains two pouches; use one 10-ounce pouch for this recipe.
  • Measure the sesame oil because one tablespoon contributes 120 to 130 calories to the batch.
  • Nutrition is approximate and varies by the leanness of the beef and product labels.
