Chicken Ranch Quest Chip Lunch Box

Prep Time: 10 minutes

Servings: 6

Serving Size: 3 ounces chicken bites | 1 bag Quest Nacho Cheese Protein Chips | 1 cup cucumber slices | 1/2 cup grapes | 1/4 cup cottage-cheese ranch

Macros Per Serving: 390 calories | 49g protein | 31g carbohydrates | 10g sugar | 7g fiber | 14g fat

Behind the Recipe

Pack it the night before and grab it on the way out. It has the crunchy chips and ranch-dip feeling of a snack lunch, but enough protein to count as an actual meal.

Ingredients

Cottage-Cheese Ranch

  • 1 1/2 cups Daisy 4% Cottage Cheese
  • 6 tablespoons 2% milk
  • 2 tablespoons ranch seasoning

Lunch Boxes

  • 18 ounces Fresh Additions Fully Cooked Chicken Breast Bites
  • 6 individual bags Quest Nacho Cheese Protein Chips
  • 6 cups sliced cucumbers
  • 3 cups seedless grapes
  • Prepared cottage-cheese ranch, divided

Instructions

  1. Blend the cottage cheese, milk, and ranch seasoning until completely smooth.
  2. Divide the ranch among 6 small sealed containers, 1/4 cup per serving.
  3. Add 3 ounces chicken, 1 cup cucumber slices, 1/2 cup grapes, and one ranch container to each divided lunch box.
  4. Keep the Quest chips sealed and outside the refrigerated lunch box until ready to eat.
  5. Pack one bag of Quest chips with each cold lunch box before leaving.

Freezer Storage

  1. Do not freeze the assembled lunch boxes, cucumbers, grapes, cottage-cheese ranch, or Quest chips.
  2. The cooked chicken may be frozen separately. If four servings remain, use a gallon-size freezer bag. If fewer than four servings remain, use a quart-size freezer bag.
  3. Remove excess air and freeze the chicken for up to 3 months.

Reheating Instructions

  1. This meal is designed to be eaten cold.
  2. If preferred, microwave the chicken separately for 45 to 60 seconds until it reaches 165°F, then return it to the lunch box.

40/400 Notes

  • Leave the chips in their original sealed bag so they stay crunchy.
  • Use a frozen lunch-pack insert if the box will be away from a refrigerator.
