Crispy Buffalo Chicken Loaded Potato Bowl

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Cook Time: 15 minutes

Servings: 1

Serving Size: 1 loaded potato bowl

Macros Per Serving: 400 calories | 42g protein | 35g carbohydrates | g sugar | g fiber | 14g fat

Behind the Recipe

This bowl has everything a loaded buffalo chicken plate should have: crisp potatoes, spicy chicken, creamy ranch, melted cheese, and fresh crunch.

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup Simply Potatoes Diced Potatoes
  • 4 ounces Realgood Foods Lightly Breaded Chicken Nuggets
  • 1/2 cup Daisy 4% Cottage Cheese
  • 1 teaspoon dry ranch seasoning
  • 1 tablespoon water
  • 1 tablespoon buffalo sauce
  • 2 tablespoons reduced-fat shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tablespoons finely diced celery
  • 1 tablespoon sliced green onion
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Nonstick cooking spray

Instructions

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 400°F.
  2. Place the diced potatoes in the air-fryer basket. Lightly coat them with cooking spray and season with the garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Air-fry the potatoes for 12 to 15 minutes, shaking the basket halfway through, until browned and crispy.
  4. Cook the chicken nuggets according to the air-fryer directions on the package. Cut the cooked chicken into bite-sized pieces and toss with the buffalo sauce.
  5. Blend the cottage cheese, ranch seasoning, and water until completely smooth and pourable.
  6. Place the crispy potatoes in a bowl. Add the buffalo chicken and sprinkle the cheddar over the hot chicken.
  7. Spoon the blended cottage-cheese ranch over the bowl. Finish with the celery and green onion.

40/400 Notes

  • Add the cottage-cheese ranch after cooking so it stays cool and creamy.
  • Nutrition varies by chicken, ranch seasoning, buffalo sauce, and cheddar brand.
