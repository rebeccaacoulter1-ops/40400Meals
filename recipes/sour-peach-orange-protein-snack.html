Sour Peach & Orange Protein Snack

Prep Time: 1 minutes

Cook Time: 0 minutes

Total Time: 1 minutes

Servings: 1

Behind the Recipe

I love keeping a few quick protein picks on hand for the days when I have no time and do not want to think about food. The Sweet Peach Punch Sour Puff satisfies a candy craving, the Tropical Orange clear protein reminds me of an Orange Julius, and the boiled egg adds something savory and filling. Having combinations like this ready makes it much easier to grab something that supports my goals instead of choosing whatever happens to be fastest.

Ingredients

  • 1 BUILT Sweet Peach Punch Sour Puff
  • 1 chilled 16-ounce can Clean Simple Eats Tropical Orange Clear Protein Water
  • 1 large hard-boiled egg

Instructions

  1. Peel the hard-boiled egg and place it on a plate or in a snack container.
  2. Add the Sweet Peach Punch Sour Puff.
  3. Serve with the chilled Tropical Orange clear protein and enjoy.

40/400 Notes

  • Keep hard-boiled eggs prepared in the refrigerator so the entire snack is ready in about one minute.
  • The sweet protein puff and orange drink satisfy a candy-like craving, while the egg adds a savory element.
  • Product nutrition can change, so use the numbers printed on your packages when tracking.
  • Nutrition is approximate and is based on one 150-calorie BUILT Sour Puff, one 90-calorie clear protein water, and one large hard-boiled egg.
