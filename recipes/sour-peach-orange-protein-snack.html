Sour Peach & Peachy Ring Protein Snack

Prep Time: 2 minutes

Servings: 1

Serving Size:

Macros Per Serving: 320 calories | 42g protein | 18g carbohydrates | g sugar | g fiber | 8g fat

Behind the Recipe

I love keeping a few quick protein picks on hand for the days when I have no time and do not want to think about food. The Sweet Peach Punch Sour Puff satisfies a candy craving, the Peachy Ring clear protein is light and refreshing, and the boiled egg adds something savory and filling. Having combinations like this ready makes it much easier to grab something that supports my goals instead of choosing whatever happens to be fastest.

Ingredients

  • 1 BUILT Sweet Peach Punch Sour Puff
  • 1 serving Clean Simple Eats Peachy Ring Clear Protein Powder
  • Cold water and ice, according to the protein powder package directions
  • 1 large hard-boiled egg

Instructions

  1. Mix the Peachy Ring clear protein powder with cold water and ice according to the package directions.
  2. Place the unopened Sweet Peach Punch Sour Puff on a small plate.
  3. Add the peeled hard-boiled egg beside the wrapped protein puff.
  4. Serve with the Peachy Ring clear protein drink and enjoy.

40/400 Notes

  • Keep hard-boiled eggs prepared in the refrigerator so the entire snack is ready in about two minutes.
  • The sour peach protein puff and Peachy Ring drink satisfy a candy-like craving, while the egg adds a savory and filling element.
  • Keep individual protein powder servings, wrapped protein puffs, and prepared eggs available for an easy no-think snack.
