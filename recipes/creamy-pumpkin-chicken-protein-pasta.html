Creamy Pumpkin Chicken Protein Pasta

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Cook Time: 18 minutes

Servings: 1

Serving Size: the full pasta mixture with 4 ounces cooked sliced chicken

Macros Per Serving: 405 calories | 45g protein | 42g carbohydrates | 5g sugar | 6g fiber | 8g fat

Behind the Recipe

Pumpkin, Greek yogurt, and Parmesan create a creamy fall sauce without heavy cream, while the chicken cooks whole to stay juicy.

Ingredients

  • 6 ounces raw boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil, divided
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 1/4 ounces dry Barilla Protein+ penne
  • 3/4 cup Simple Truth Chicken Bone Broth
  • 1/3 cup pure pumpkin purée
  • 1/4 cup Chobani Nonfat Plain Greek Yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 teaspoon rubbed sage

Instructions

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 375°F.
  2. Rub the whole chicken breast with 1/2 teaspoon olive oil, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Air-fry for 10 to 14 minutes, turning halfway, until the thickest part reaches 165°F. Rest for 5 minutes before slicing.
  3. While the chicken cooks, add the dry pasta, bone broth, and remaining 1/2 teaspoon olive oil to a small saucepan. Bring to a gentle boil and cook uncovered, stirring often, until the pasta is tender and only a few tablespoons of liquid remain.
  4. Reduce the heat to low. Stir in the pumpkin, Greek yogurt, Parmesan, and sage until smooth and warmed through. Do not boil after adding the yogurt.
  5. Transfer the full pasta mixture to a shallow bowl or wide plate and top with exactly 4 ounces sliced cooked chicken.

40/400 Notes

  • Cook the chicken breast whole and slice it only after the five-minute rest.
  • Add 1 to 2 tablespoons water if the pasta needs more liquid before it becomes tender.
  • Pumpkin should taste savory here; do not add cinnamon or sweetener.
