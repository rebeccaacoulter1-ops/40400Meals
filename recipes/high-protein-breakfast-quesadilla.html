High-Protein Breakfast Quesadilla

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Cook Time: 10 minutes

Total Time: 15 minutes

Servings: 1

Behind the Recipe

This is the kind of breakfast that actually holds you until lunch. Blended cottage cheese makes the eggs creamy, while chicken sausage and a crisp tortilla make it feel like a real breakfast instead of a sad little protein project.

Ingredients

  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 cup Simple Truth Organic Cage Free 100% Liquid Egg Whites
  • 1/4 cup Daisy 4% Cottage Cheese
  • 2 Amylu Chicken Breakfast Sausage Links from the Costco club pack, chopped
  • 1 Mission Carb Balance Soft Taco Flour Tortilla
  • 2 tablespoons shredded reduced-fat cheddar cheese
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Pinch of salt and black pepper
  • Nonstick cooking spray

Instructions

  1. Blend the egg, egg whites, cottage cheese, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until mostly smooth.
  2. Coat a nonstick skillet with cooking spray and place it over medium heat.
  3. Add the chopped chicken sausage and cook for 2 minutes, until warmed and lightly browned.
  4. Pour in the egg mixture and gently scramble until just set, 3 to 4 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
  5. Place the tortilla in the skillet. Spoon the egg and sausage mixture over one half, sprinkle with cheddar, and fold the tortilla over.
  6. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes per side, pressing gently, until crisp and the cheese has melted.
  7. Rest for 1 minute, then cut into three wedges.

40/400 Notes

  • Keep the heat at medium so the tortilla crisps without burning.
  • The filling can be made the night before and reheated before assembling.
  • Nutrition is approximate and varies by sausage, tortilla, cottage cheese, and cheddar brand.
