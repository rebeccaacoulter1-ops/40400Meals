Chili Lime Chips, Guac & Protein Snack

Prep Time: 2 minutes

Servings: 1

Serving Size:

Macros Per Serving: 330 calories | 41g protein | 13g carbohydrates | g sugar | g fiber | 13g fat

Behind the Recipe

I always keep a few quick picks on hand because the hardest food decisions happen when I am busy, hungry, and do not want to think. Chili Lime protein chips with a guacamole cup feel like chips and dip, while the strawberry coconut-style clear protein brings the snack above 40 grams of protein without making it feel like a full meal. It is crunchy, refreshing, satisfying, and ready in about two minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 bag Quest Chili Lime Tortilla Style Protein Chips
  • 1 Kirkland Signature Organic Chunky Guacamole cup, 2.5 ounces
  • 1 serving Clean Simple Eats Strawberry Piña Colada Clear Protein Powder
  • Cold water and ice, according to the protein powder package directions

Instructions

  1. Mix the Strawberry Piña Colada clear protein with cold water according to the package directions, adding ice if desired.
  2. Open the protein chips and guacamole cup.
  3. Serve the chips with the guacamole for dipping and enjoy with the clear protein drink.

40/400 Notes

  • Keep the guacamole cups refrigerated and the chips and protein packets together for a true grab-and-go option.
  • The chili lime chips and creamy guacamole provide the salty, crunchy part of the snack while the clear protein adds a refreshing sweet finish.
  • Use a different clear protein flavor when desired without changing the chips-and-guacamole combination.
  • Product nutrition can change, so use the numbers printed on your packages when tracking.
  • Nutrition is approximate and is based on one 140-calorie bag of Quest chips, one 90-calorie guacamole cup, and one 100-calorie serving of clear protein powder.
