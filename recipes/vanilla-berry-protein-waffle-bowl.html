Vanilla Berry Protein Waffle Bowl

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Cook Time: 3 minutes

Total Time: 8 minutes

Servings: 1

Behind the Recipe

This is the kind of breakfast that feels fun without requiring any real cooking. The protein powder turns the yogurt into a thicker vanilla dip, and the two warm protein waffles make it easy to scoop and dip.

Ingredients

  • 2 Eggo Protein Buttermilk Vanilla Waffles
  • 1 cup Oikos Triple Zero Vanilla Greek Yogurt
  • 1/2 scoop Clean Simple Eats Simply Vanilla Protein Powder
  • 1/2 cup fresh strawberries, sliced

Instructions

  1. Toast the waffles according to package directions.
  2. Stir the protein powder into the Greek yogurt until smooth and creamy.
  3. Top the yogurt with the sliced strawberries.
  4. Serve the two warm whole waffles alongside the yogurt bowl for dipping.

40/400 Notes

  • Serve the waffles whole beside the yogurt bowl for a simple, realistic one-serving presentation.
  • Nutrition is approximate and may vary by yogurt and protein powder formulation.
