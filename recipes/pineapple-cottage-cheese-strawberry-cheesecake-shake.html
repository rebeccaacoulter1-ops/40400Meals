Pineapple Cottage Cheese Bowl + Strawberry Cheesecake Shake

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Servings: 1

Serving Size:

Macros Per Serving: 355 calories | 42g protein | 29g carbohydrates | g sugar | g fiber | 12g fat

Behind the Recipe

This is a no-cook breakfast for mornings when speed matters. The cottage cheese and pineapple stay simple, while the strawberry cheesecake shake adds enough protein to turn the combination into a full 40/400 meal.

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup Daisy 4% Cottage Cheese
  • 1/2 cup Dole pineapple, drained
  • 1 scoop Clean Simple Eats Strawberry Cheesecake Protein Powder
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • Ice, as needed

Some links above are Amazon affiliate links — as an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. See our disclosure.

Instructions

  1. Spoon the cottage cheese into a bowl and top with the drained pineapple.
  2. Blend the Strawberry Cheesecake protein powder, unsweetened almond milk, and ice until smooth.
  3. Serve the cottage cheese bowl and protein shake together.

40/400 Notes

  • Drain the pineapple before measuring for the closest match to the listed nutrition.
  • Use unsweetened almond milk to leave more room under 400 calories.
  • Nutrition is approximate and may vary by almond milk and protein powder formulation.
