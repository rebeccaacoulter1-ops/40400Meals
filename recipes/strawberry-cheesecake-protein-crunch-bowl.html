Strawberry Cheesecake Protein Crunch Bowl

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Servings: 1

Serving Size: 1 protein crunch bowl

Macros Per Serving: 340 calories | 42g protein | 38g carbohydrates | g sugar | g fiber | 3g fat

Behind the Recipe

One scoop of strawberry cheesecake protein turns plain Greek yogurt into a five-minute dessert-style bowl without pudding mix or cottage cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Chobani Nonfat Plain Greek Yogurt
  • 1 scoop Clean Simple Eats Strawberry Cheesecake Protein Powder
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 1 cup sliced fresh strawberries
  • 1 full Honey Maid Graham Cracker sheet, crushed

Instructions

  1. Stir or blend the Greek yogurt, protein powder, and water until completely smooth.
  2. Spoon the cheesecake mixture into a bowl.
  3. Top with the sliced strawberries.
  4. Crush the graham cracker over the bowl immediately before serving.

40/400 Notes

  • For meal prep, store the graham cracker separately and add it immediately before serving.
  • Nutrition varies by yogurt, protein powder, and graham cracker brand.
