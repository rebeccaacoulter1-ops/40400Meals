Warm Cinnamon Apple Cheesecake Protein Parfait

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Cook Time: 6 minutes

Servings: 1

Serving Size: 1 clear glass dessert dish, about 1 1/2 cups

Macros Per Serving: 410 calories | 43g protein | 24g carbohydrates | 9g sugar | 4g fiber | 18g fat

Behind the Recipe

This fall parfait gives warm apple-cheesecake flavor while keeping the layers simple, measurable, and protein-forward.

Ingredients

  • 2 ounces apple, cored and finely diced
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon apple pie spice
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 tablespoon water
  • 1 cup Chobani Zero Sugar Vanilla Greek Yogurt
  • 1 scoop Clean Simple Eats Vanilla Protein Powder
  • 2 tablespoons almond butter, divided
  • 2 tablespoons water, plus more only if needed

Instructions

  1. Add the diced apple, cinnamon, apple pie spice, vanilla, salt, and 1 tablespoon water to a small skillet over medium heat.
  2. Cook for 5 to 6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the apple is warm and fork-tender but still holds its shape. Remove from the heat and cool for 3 minutes.
  3. Stir the Greek yogurt, protein powder, 1 1/2 tablespoons almond butter, and 2 tablespoons water until completely smooth and thick. Add only enough extra water to reach a cheesecake-like consistency.
  4. Spoon half of the cheesecake mixture into a clear glass dessert dish and add half of the warm apples.
  5. Repeat the layers, then drizzle the remaining 1/2 tablespoon almond butter over the top.
  6. Serve immediately while the apples are warm.

40/400 Notes

  • Use a clear glass dessert dish so the cheesecake and apple layers remain visible.
  • For meal prep, refrigerate the yogurt mixture and cooked apples separately and assemble immediately before serving.
  • Weigh the edible apple portion to 2 ounces to keep the listed sugar and macros consistent.
