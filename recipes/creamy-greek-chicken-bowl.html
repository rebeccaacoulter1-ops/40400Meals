Creamy Greek Chicken Bowl

Prep Time: 10 minutes

Cook Time: 0 minutes

Total Time: 10 minutes

Servings: 1

Behind the Recipe

This is one of those bowls I make by feel because the ingredients are simple and the flavors do the work. A full pack of grilled chicken makes the protein easy, and the creamy Greek dressing comes together with nonfat Greek yogurt, red wine vinegar, a tiny bit of olive oil, and Cavender's Greek seasoning. It tastes like a real lunch, not a diet meal, and most of the ingredients are useful for other meals too.

Ingredients

  • 1 Fresh Additions fully cooked chicken breast bites pack, 100 grams
  • 2 cups leafy green lettuce
  • 1/2 cup chopped cucumber
  • 1/2 cup chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons thinly sliced red onion
  • 4 Mezzetta pitted Greek Kalamata olives, sliced
  • 1 ounce President crumbled feta cheese
  • 1/2 cup Kirkland Signature Organic plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 2 teaspoons red wine vinegar
  • 1/2 teaspoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon Cavender's All Purpose Greek Seasoning
  • 1 to 2 teaspoons water, as needed to thin the dressing

Instructions

  1. Add the leafy greens to a large bowl and top with the cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, Kalamata olives, feta, and the full pack of grilled chicken breast bites.
  2. In a small bowl, stir together the Greek yogurt, red wine vinegar, olive oil, and Cavender's Greek seasoning until creamy.
  3. Add 1 to 2 teaspoons of water if needed to thin the dressing to your preferred consistency.
  4. Spoon the creamy Greek dressing over the bowl, toss lightly, and serve.

40/400 Notes

  • The full individual chicken pack keeps the portion simple and makes this an easy workday lunch.
  • Start with a small amount of water in the dressing and add more only if needed.
  • The tiny amount of olive oil adds flavor and richness without taking over the calorie budget.
