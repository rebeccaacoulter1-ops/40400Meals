# 40/400 Production Roadmap

For each 20-workday production cycle, publish six recipes per day: 30 breakfasts, 30 lunches, 30 dinners, and 30 snacks.

- Odd production days: 2 breakfasts, 1 lunch, 2 dinners, 1 snack.
- Even production days: 1 breakfast, 2 lunches, 1 dinner, 2 snacks.
- Weekly target: 30 recipes and at least 30 Pinterest pins.
- Monthly target: 120 recipes and at least 120 Pinterest pins.
- Batch recipes use 1-2 tablespoons each of garlic powder and onion powder.

After publishing, Bear records only verified recipe and Pinterest-pin counts in the Google dashboard. Traffic, subscribers, and revenue remain untouched until their source services are connected.
