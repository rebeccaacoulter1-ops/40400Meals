Three days. Three meals. One simple way to start.

You do not need a perfect meal-prep Sunday or a refrigerator full of specialty food. Start with one high-protein meal a day that is easy enough to repeat.

One grocery list for all three meals

Check your pantry first, then shop once for the full three-day plan.

Protein and dairy

  • Boneless skinless chicken breast
  • Lean ground beef
  • Plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • Grated Parmesan cheese

Produce

  • Romaine lettuce
  • 1 medium sweet potato
  • Fresh or steam-in-bag broccoli
  • Garlic

Refrigerated and pantry

  • High-protein or carb-balanced wraps
  • Protein penne
  • Light Caesar dressing
  • Lower-sugar Alfredo sauce

Seasonings and extras

  • Olive oil or avocado-oil spray
  • Garlic powder and onion powder
  • Italian seasoning
  • Salt and pepper

Make the three days easier

  • Cook or buy enough chicken for more than one meal so tomorrow starts easier.
  • Wash and portion produce when you get home from the store.
  • Keep one high-protein convenience option available for the day that goes sideways.
  • Repeat meals you genuinely like. Consistency beats novelty.

Your only goal

Do these three meals once. Notice which one you would happily eat again. That is how a sustainable personal menu starts: not with 100 recipes, but with a few dependable winners.
