YOUTUBE SHORT HOOK:
This creamy chicken Alfredo cooks in one pot and still fits the 40/400 method.

SCRIPT:
- Cook the chicken first, then move it to a bowl.
- Cook protein penne directly in chicken bone broth until the broth is absorbed.
- Add broccoli near the end, then stir in a little heavy cream and Parmesan.
- Fold the chicken back in and dinner is done.
